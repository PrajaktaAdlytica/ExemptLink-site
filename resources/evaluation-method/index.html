The decision path

Evidence before expansion.

01Current coverage

Capture suite alerts and baseline effort.

02Tax-owner review

Classify gaps, false warnings and omissions.

03Incremental value

Confirm new, remediable findings.

04Commercial decision

Agree a signed and funded pilot.

Acceptance criteria

Proceed on reviewed evidence.

Previously unidentified, remediable gaps; tax-owner sign-off; a bounded paid audit-prep pilot; agreed access and responsibility.

Kill criteria

Stop if the job is already closed.

The incumbent achieves equivalent coverage at lower total review cost, findings cannot be verified, or the buyer will not commit to a funded scope.

Expansion is conditional.

Resale and industry-specific exemptions require further jurisdiction research. VAT is a later hypothesis, outside this starting scope.
