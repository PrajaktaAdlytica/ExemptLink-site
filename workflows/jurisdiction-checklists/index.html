Workflow questions

Know the boundary.

Do all certificates expire annually?

No. Do not assign a universal expiry to New York blanket certificates. The separate Florida annual resale example demonstrates a different rule.

Does a complete checklist establish legal validity?

No. Completeness is one review input. The transaction facts, form and interpretation still need tax-owner review.

Can this workflow change returns or source systems?

No. The starting scope is permissioned historical evidence and human review. The preview does not execute outbound actions or system writes.
