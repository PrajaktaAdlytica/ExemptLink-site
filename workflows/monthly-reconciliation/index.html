Workflow questions

Know the boundary.

What does the USD 900 example mean?

The fictional engine export omits TX-096. GL and evidence sample totals are USD 16,000; the engine total is USD 15,100. The difference needs review.

Is this included automatically in the first pilot?

No. Each workflow is tested independently. Reconciliation additionally requires properly bounded monthly exports.

Can this workflow change returns or source systems?

No. The starting scope is permissioned historical evidence and human review. The preview does not execute outbound actions or system writes.
