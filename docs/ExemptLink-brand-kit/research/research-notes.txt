VISUAL INTERPRETATION
An exemption-evidence brand benefits from exact relationships, readable boundaries and inspectable source artifacts. The selected Atlas direction uses state seams, registration marks, source brackets and human review gates. It avoids literal US flags, eagles, seals, shields, chain links, generic AI orbs and financial-growth charts.
The selected Seam logo connects geography and reviewable linkage without implying a government designation.

INTERACTION PRINCIPLES
1. Visible keyboard focus: use focus-visible while preserving actual focus indication. Shutter and icon exchange also respond to keyboard focus.
https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/:focus-visible
2. Hover support varies: use hover/pointer media conditions. Essential content remains visible on touch; magnetic movement is restricted to a fine pointer.
https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/@media/hover
https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/@media/pointer
3. Respect reduced motion: show immediate states and a readable still composition. The kit also provides a manual still switch.
https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/@media/prefers-reduced-motion
4. Required text contrast is at least 4.5:1 for normal text; necessary graphical/control boundaries at least 3:1. Calculated key pairs are delivered, not a site-wide conformance certificate.
https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html
https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html
5. Avoid revealing essential instructions only on hover. If later popovers show content on hover/focus, account for dismissibility, persistence and pointer travel.
https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/content-on-hover-or-focus.html
6. Prefer transform/color/stroke changes that preserve the control's layout. SVG stroke tracing is suitable for finite evidence connectors.
https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/SVG/Reference/Attribute/stroke-dasharray
https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/SVG/Reference/Attribute/stroke-dashoffset
